## Proactive computing

Do you know why Cursor's tab is so popular? Because is so well done that it seems like an oracle. It's always two or three steps ahead of you, and the best part, you don't even have to ask for it.

This concept is called `proactive computing`. It can be applied to UI/UX, AI, and more. You may have already experienced it in your daily life without even realizing it.

Examples:
- Recommendation algorithms present in every major social media platform.
- Frontend frameworks like Next.js that prefetch the next page you are likely to visit.

Mercado Libre (Mercado Pago), for example, takes this concept to the next level:
Let's suppose we are in the supermarket. They detect your location with surgical precision. As soon as you open Mercado Pago app, it will show a popup asking if you want to transfer money to the supermarket bank account. 

The point is: **read the user mindset and predict their needs before they even ask or think about it**.
